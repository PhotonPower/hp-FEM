std::optional<LayerStack<2> > hpfem::physics::ConicalScatteringSetup::background

◆ incident_curl

ConicalField hpfem::physics::ConicalScatteringSetup::incident_curl

Physical curl of the physical incident field, \( (\partial_yE_z - i\beta E_y,\ i\beta E_x - \partial_xE_z,\ \partial_xE_y - \partial_yE_x) \), used by h_field and poynting of the total field: conical_plane_wave_curl or LayeredConicalWave::field_curl. If empty, incident_curl falls back to central differences of incident (relative accuracy of about 1e-8).

◆ keep_factorisation

bool hpfem::physics::ConicalScatteringSetup::keep_factorisation = false

Keep the factorised system in the solution (ConicalSolution::factorisation) for adjoint and tangent solves of the sensitivities (ADR-0012): no second assembly and factorisation, at the price of holding the factors as long as the solution lives.

◆ scalar_ez

bool hpfem::physics::ConicalScatteringSetup::scalar_ez = false

Scalar E_z path: at β = 0 the block system decouples and an E_z-only excitation (the s polarisation at φ = 0, or a current along z) leaves the in-plane field zero, so only the H1 block (about a third of the DoFs) is factorised and solved; the in-plane coefficients of the solution are zero and every post-processing applies unchanged. The constructor checks β = 0 and that the incident field / current has no in-plane components. Opt-in; hpfem.grating.solve sets it automatically for s at φ = 0.
