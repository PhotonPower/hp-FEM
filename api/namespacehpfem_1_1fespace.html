Scaled integrated Legendre polynomials \( L_i^S(x, t) = t^i L_i(x/t) \), i = 1..n, with \( L_1 = x \) and \( L_i(x) = \int_{-1}^{x} P_{i-1}(s)\,ds \) for i ≥ 2 (so that \( L_i(\pm 1) = 0 \)). Homogeneous of degree i in (x, t); for t = 1 they are the plain integrated Legendre polynomials. Entry 0 of every output is unused (set to 0); values, dx, dt must have size n + 1.
