Detailed Description

Power of an order-m field through the disc r ≤ radius at height z (aperture transmission, ADR-0014 §4): total of the field plus the added analytic field, background of the added field alone through the same facets, both along direction (−1: downwards, the transmitted power of a wave from the top is positive; +1: upwards). change() is the transmission caused by the body; summed over the orders, background is the stack's transmittance times the incident power on the disc. [W].
