Detailed Description

template<int Dim>
class hpfem::physics::CyclicSymmetricFarField< Dim >

Far field of the scattered field of the full problem from a closed surface of the sector mesh in the homogeneous background: per order the FarField of the reduced surface (facets on the sides dropped), and \( F(\hat r) = \sum_m\sum_j \omega^{mj}\,e^{-ik\hat r\cdot t_j}\,R^j F_m(R^{-j}\hat r) \) with \( t_j = c - R^jc \).
