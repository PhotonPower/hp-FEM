Detailed Description

Dipole emitters in periodic structures (M17, ADR-0013): the source of one cell problem of the array scanning and the power it delivers. A point dipole of current moment p [A m] at \( x_0 \), smeared over the normalised 3D Gaussian of M11, \( g = e^{-|x - x_0|^2/2\sigma^2}/((2\pi)^{3/2}\sigma^3) \), decomposes over the Bloch wavenumber \( k_x \) and the longitudinal wavenumber β into the cell sources

\[ J_{k_x,\beta}(x, y) = p\, g_2(x - x_0, y - y_0)\, e^{-\sigma^2\beta^2/2}, \qquad g_2 = e^{-\rho^2/2\sigma^2}/(2\pi\sigma^2), \]

one per cell with the Bloch phase \( e^{ik_xP} \) (the z-smearing is the Fourier factor of the β sample). The single dipole is recovered by \( E = \frac{P}{2\pi}\int_{BZ}dk_x\,\frac1{2\pi}\int d\beta\,E_{k_x,\beta}\,e^{i\beta z} \), its power by the same integral of the cell power \( P_{cell} = -\tfrac12\mathrm{Re}\int_{cell} \bar J_{k_x,\beta}\cdot E_{k_x,\beta}\,dA \) [W/m per unit β]. Convention exp(-iωt). See docs/theory/maxwell.md#dipole-emitters.
