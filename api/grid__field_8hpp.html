Detailed Description

A scalar volume field on a regular grid, interpolated linearly per axis (bilinear in 2D, trilinear in 3D): the input path of volume data ports such as the shrinkage or the degree of polymerisation of a printed part (ADR-0016 §5, hpfem.dataports.RegularGrid). An axis of length 1 means invariance along it. Lengths in metres.
