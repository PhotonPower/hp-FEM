Detailed Description

Scattering problems with mirror symmetry (issue #244, S3): a structure that is invariant under the reflections at k = 1 … Dim mutually orthogonal planes is computed on the part of the domain on one side of every plane, for any excitation. The excitation (incident field and current) is split into its 2^k parity parts \( E_\sigma = 2^{-k}\sum_g \chi_\sigma(g)\,R_g E(A_g x) \) (g runs over the products of the reflections \( A_g x = R_g x + t_g \), \( \chi_\sigma(g) \) is the product of the signs \( \sigma_j = \pm 1 \) of the reflections in g); part σ is solved on the reduced domain with a PEC wall ( \( n\times E = 0 \)) on the planes where it is odd (σ_j = −1) and the natural magnetic wall ( \( n\times H = 0 \)) where it is even, and the field of the full domain is \( E(x) = \sum_\sigma \chi_\sigma(g)\,R_g E_\sigma(A_g x) \) with the g that folds x into the reduced domain. Parts whose excitation vanishes are not solved (an excitation with the symmetry of the structure needs one solve). Powers are quadratic and the parts orthogonal on symmetric surfaces and volumes, so fluxes and absorbed powers are 2^k times the sum over the parts on the reduced domain; the far field sums the images of every part. Convention exp(−iωt). See docs/theory/maxwell.md#mirror-symmetry-physicsmirror_symmetryhpp.
