◆ background

template<int Dim>
std::optional<LayerStack<Dim> > hpfem::physics::ScatteringSetup< Dim >::background

Layered background (ADR-0009): the incident field is then the stack's plane wave (LayerStack::plane_wave(...).field), the scattered-field source lives only where a cell's material deviates from the stack at the cell centroid, and cross-sections are normalised by the incidence medium. Layer interfaces must coincide with facets.
