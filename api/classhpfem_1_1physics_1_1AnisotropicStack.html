Detailed Description

A planar stack of possibly anisotropic layers (laboratory frame, z the normal): isotropic, lossless incidence medium above top, layers from top to bottom, semi-infinite substrate.

The analytic field of the downward mode mode (0 or 1, the order of half_space_modes: s / p, ordinary / extraordinary) of the incidence medium, with |E| = amplitude [V/m], at the normalised in-plane wavevector (a, b): as plane_wave, also for a lossless crystal incidence medium (M19 S7e: the plane wave incident from a crystal substrate, mirrored, in the reciprocity of emitters). response is left empty. Plane wave of polarisation pol (s or p) and amplitude |E| = amplitude [V/m] incident as in response: the analytic field in every region (E and curl E = i k0 Z0 H), \( \psi(z) = W_u e^{ik_0T_u(z - z_b)}\alpha + W_d e^{ik_0T_d(z - z_t)}\delta \) with the upward amplitudes at the bottom and the downward ones at the top of each region, so all exponentials decay.

◆ reflection_at()

Eigen::Matrix2cd hpfem::physics::AnisotropicStack::reflection_at ( Real  k0,
Complex  a,
Complex  b 
) const

Jones reflection matrix (as StackResponse::reflection) at the normalised in-plane wavevector (a, b) = (k_x, k_y)/k0 of the incidence medium, also beyond its light cone, where the incident and the reflected wave are evanescent (k_z / k0 = ±sqrt(n0² − a² − b²), Im ≥ 0 towards the decaying side): the plane-wave spectrum of a dipole above the stack (dipole_power_above). ŝ = ẑ × k̂_∥ (ŷ at a = b = 0), p̂ = ŝ × k̂ / n0 with the complex k̂. Guided modes of a lossless stack are poles on the real (a, b) plane, where the matrix is singular or large.

Exceptions
InvalidArgumentfor k0 ≤ 0 or grazing propagation in a region (k_z = 0, e.g. |(a, b)| = n0). (a, b) may be complex: the analytic continuation on a deformed integration path below the real axis (guided-mode poles of lossless stacks, M19 S7e), with ŝ normalised by the non-conjugating norm and the up / down modes of every region chosen by Im k_z (causal).
