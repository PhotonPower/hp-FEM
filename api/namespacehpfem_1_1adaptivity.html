Dörfler (bulk) marking: the smallest set \( \mathcal M \) of cells, taken in descending order of the indicator, with \( \sum_{K\in\mathcal M}\eta_K^2 \ge \theta\sum_K\eta_K^2 \). Returns the marked cell ids ascending; empty if all indicators vanish. O(N log N).

◆ dual_form()

template<int Dim>
DualForm< Dim > hpfem::adaptivity::dual_form ( const assembly::MaxwellForm< Dim > &  primal,
Real  k_squared 
)

The dual form of one cell: coefficients \( (k^2\varepsilon)^{-1} \) (stiffness), \( \mu \) (mass, entering as permittivity with the mass coefficient 1 in 3D and as the reaction −μ in 2D), the sources \( \mu g \) and \( (k^2\varepsilon)^{-1} f \) (paired with the curl of the test function). Empty coefficient functions of primal mean the identity (μ⁻¹, ε) or zero (f, g).

Exceptions
InvalidArgumentif k² is zero.

Galerkin solution of the dual problem on dual_dofs for the per-cell primal forms and the mass coefficient k² of the primal problem. essential_facets are the facets where the primal problem has the natural (PMC) condition: there the dual field has the essential one (tangential trace zero in 3D, value zero in 2D). Where the primal field is PEC nothing is imposed on the dual field. Constrained meshes (hanging nodes, Bloch) are not supported.

Exceptions
InvalidArgumentif k² is zero.

◆ hp_refine()

template<int Dim>
HpStep hpfem::adaptivity::hp_refine ( mesh::AdaptiveMesh< Dim > &  mesh,
std::span< const int >  orders,
std::span< const Index >  h_marked,
std::span< const Index >  p_marked,
int  increment = 1,
int  max_order = 0,
bool  spread_p = true 
)

Refines h_marked cells in the hierarchy (children inherit the parent's order, cells refined for the one-irregular closure as well) and raises the order of p_marked cells (a cell in both lists is h-refined and its children raised). With spread_p, facet neighbours of a p-marked cell (across hanging facets too) whose order is below the cell's new order are raised by increment as well, unless they are h-marked: under the minimum rule the shared edges and faces would otherwise stay at the neighbour's order and the raised cell would gain interior functions only. Both lists refer to the leaf mesh before the call; orders has one entry per leaf cell.

Exceptions
InvalidArgumentfor a mismatch of orders and the mesh or a cell out of range.

Indicators of the primal coefficients e_h on dofs and the dual coefficients sigma_h on dual_dofs (same mesh) for the per-cell forms and the mass coefficient k². The weighted norms are \( |w^H T w| \) with the energy weights \( T = \mu \) and \( T = -k^2\varepsilon \), which are the exact energy norms for real coefficients and k² < 0 and their moduli otherwise (lossy materials, PML). Quadrature of degree 2p + extra_order (+2 on curved cells).

Exceptions
InvalidArgumentif a coefficient vector does not match its map or the maps have different meshes.

Weighted residual for goal-oriented estimation: the cell contributions \( r_K(w) = \int_K R_K\cdot w + \tfrac12\sum_{F\subset\partial K}\int_F (n\times[\![w_h]\!])\cdot w \) (no conjugation, signed) of a weight \( w \) given as coefficients on weight_dofs (same mesh, e.g. the orders raised by one); boundary facets contribute their one-sided term \( \int_F (n imes w_h)\cdot w \) to their cell (the residual of a natural condition), so that \( \sum_K r_K(w) = \ell(w) - a(E_h, w) \) for every tangentially continuous \( w \).
