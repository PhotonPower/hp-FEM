Detailed Description

The two upward or the two downward plane-wave modes of a homogeneous half-space at the normalised in-plane wavevector (a, b) = (k_x, k_y)/k0, labelled as the substrate modes of StackResponse (s / p, ordinary / extraordinary, biaxial, power-orthogonal where they coincide). Each mode has unit |E|; the isotropic s vector is \( \hat z\times\hat k_\parallel \) (ŷ at a = b = 0) and p = s × k̂ / n.
