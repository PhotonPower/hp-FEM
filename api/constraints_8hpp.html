Detailed Description

Linear constraints between degrees of freedom, \( x_s = \sum_i c_i\, x_{m_i} \) (slave = combination of masters with complex coefficients): Bloch-periodic boundaries now, hanging nodes of irregular refinement later (docs/theory/hp-adaptivity.md). Chains (a master that is itself a slave) are resolved by substitution; cycles are rejected. Constraints are applied to an assembled system by the prolongation \( x = P x_f \) of the free DoFs: \( A_f = P^H A P \), \( b_f = P^H b \). The conjugate transpose selects the test functions with the conjugate coefficients: for Bloch phases \( e^{ik\cdot a} \) with real k the boundary terms of the weak form cancel only against test functions of phase \( e^{-ik\cdot a} \) (for real coefficients, hanging nodes, it is the plain transpose). See docs/theory/maxwell.md#bloch-periodic-constraints.
