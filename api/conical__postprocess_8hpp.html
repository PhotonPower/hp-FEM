Detailed Description

Post-processing of the conical solver (M15 F10 / F11): diffraction orders on any line with complex vector amplitudes, the flux-based power balance, cross-sections of isolated scatterers and the far-field pattern of the 2.5D field, plus the frame conversion to the grating literature. All fields are physical \( (E_x, E_y, E_z) \) of \( E(x,y)e^{i\beta z} \); the solver frame has the period along x, the normal along y and the invariant direction z, the literature frame (BEM / RCWA) the period along x', the invariant direction y' and the normal z': \( (x', y', z') = (x, -z, y) \) (right-handed). Convention exp(-iωt). See docs/theory/maxwell.md#conical-incidence-and-the-e_z-polarisation.
