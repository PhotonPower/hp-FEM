Detailed Description

template<int Dim>
class hpfem::pml::RadialPml< Dim >

PML of radial shape (issue #244 S4, docs/theory/pml.md "Radial layers"): beyond the distance \( r_0 \) from the centre (circle, sphere) or from the axis (cylinder) the distance is continued as \( \tilde r = r + i\int_{r_0}^{r}\hat\sigma \) with the profile of PmlProfile, and the point as \( \tilde x = c + (\tilde r/r)(x - c) \) (cylinder: the part perpendicular to the axis). The Jacobian \( J = s_r\,\hat r\hat r^T + (\tilde r/r)(P - \hat r\hat r^T) + (I - P) \) ( \( P \) the projector onto the stretched plane or space, \( s_r = 1 + i\hat\sigma(r) \)) is a full symmetric tensor in Cartesian components; the materials become \( \tilde\varepsilon = \det J\,J^{-1}\varepsilon J^{-T} \) and \( \tilde\mu^{-1} = J\mu^{-1}J^T/\det J \) exactly as for PmlBox. Unlike the box, the layer is invariant under every rotation about the centre (axis), so a sector of a structure with cyclic symmetry C_n of any order can carry it. The cylinder takes, in addition, slab layers on the two ends of an interval along the axis (as a PmlBox would). Points beyond the layer keep the final stretch \( \hat\sigma_{\max} \) (a square mesh around a circular layer is allowed). Convention exp(−iωt): the imaginary part of the stretch is positive.

InvalidArgumentfor a zero axis, axial_lower ≥ axial_upper, negative axial thickness or the conditions of the constructor.
