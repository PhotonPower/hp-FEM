Detailed Description

Linear functionals on the Nédélec space as vectors: \( Q(E_h) = q^\top e_h \) with \( q_i = Q(\phi_i) \) (no conjugation, like the bilinear forms). Point-sampled functionals \( Q(E) = \sum_j E(x_j)\cdot w_j + (\nabla\times E)(x_j)\cdot v_j \) cover point values, line and surface integrals by quadrature (Fourier coefficients of diffraction orders, far-field patterns). Used as goals of the dual-weighted residual estimator (physics/goal_oriented.hpp).
