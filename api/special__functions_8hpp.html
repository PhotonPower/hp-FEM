Detailed Description

Cylindrical Bessel and Hankel functions of real argument (line sources, Mie series for the cylinder) and spherical Bessel functions of real and complex argument (Mie series for the sphere). Thin wrappers around the C++17 special functions of the standard library (available in libstdc++ and the MSVC STL, not in libc++). Convention exp(−iωt): \( H_n^{(1)} = J_n + iY_n \) is outgoing.
