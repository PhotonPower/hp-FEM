Detailed Description

Eigenproblems of a body of revolution (2.5D): for the azimuthal order m the Maxwell eigenproblem \( \nabla\times(\mu_r^{-1}\nabla\times E) = k_0^2\varepsilon_r E \) with \( E = E_m(r, z) e^{im\varphi} \) is posed on the meridian mesh (x = r, y = z) with the forms of assembly::assemble_axisymmetric, PEC walls (tangential trace of \( (E_r, E_z) \) and \( E_\varphi \) zero) and the axis conditions that keep the field regular at r = 0: \( v = -i r E_\varphi = 0 \) for every m and \( E_z = 0 \) for m ≠ 0 (both as Dirichlet data on the axis facets). The gradient kernel is removed with the order-m gradient \( (\nabla\psi, m\psi) \) as in the Cartesian cavity solver, so no spurious modes appear. AxisymmetricCavity solves the closed (lossless) problem, AxisymmetricResonance the open one with the cylindrical PML (quasi-normal modes with complex ω, Q factor). See docs/theory/axisymmetric.md and ADR-0010.
