Detailed Description

template<int Dim>
struct hpfem::physics::AbsorptionDensity< Dim >

The Joule heating at the quadrature points of all lossy cells: physical point, quadrature weight including the Jacobian ([m^Dim]), density Q ([W/m^3], [W/m^2] in 2D) and cell; \( \sum_q w_q Q_q \) is the absorbed power.
