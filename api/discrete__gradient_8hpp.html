Detailed Description

The discrete gradient G: coefficients of the H1 space → coefficients of the Nédélec space such that ∇(Σ u_i φ_i^{H1}) = Σ (G u)_j φ_j^{ND} exactly (docs/theory/nedelec.md#gauging). Because the hierarchical Nédélec basis contains the gradients of the H1 basis explicitly, G has entries ±1 (Whitney functions of the edges at a vertex) and 1 (higher functions).
