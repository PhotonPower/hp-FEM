Detailed Description

Polynomial absorption profile \( \hat\sigma(d) = \hat\sigma_{\max}(d/\text{thickness})^m \) with \( \hat\sigma_{\max} = -\frac{(m+1)\ln R_0}{2\,k_0\,n\,\text{thickness}} \), so that a wave at normal incidence returns from the far end with amplitude ratio \( R_0 \). At the angle \( \theta \) against the layer normal the attenuation scales with \( \cos\theta \): the round trip leaves \( R_0^{\cos\theta} \) and the far wall receives the one-way field \( R_0^{\cos\theta/2} \), so a reflectance computed with the layer is wrong by up to \( 2|r|R_0^{\cos\theta/2} \) ( \( r \) the reflection amplitude of the structure). Choose \( R_0 \) for the largest angle that occurs (for_angle); the thickness only sets how steep the profile is (docs/theory/pml.md, "Oblique incidence").

\( R_0 \) such that the reflectance error of the layer stays below target up to the incidence angle theta_max [rad] against the layer normal for a structure of reflection amplitude r_amplitude: \( R_0 = (\text{target} / (2 r))^{2/\cos\theta_{\max}} \), clamped to \( [10^{-300}, 1) \). Steep profiles need thick layers: check PmlBox::max_resolution or use PmlBox::recommended_thickness with the profile.
