Detailed Description

template<int Dim>
struct hpfem::assembly::MaxwellForm< Dim >

Coefficients of the Maxwell forms; an empty function means the identity (ε = μ = 1, i.e. dimensionless vacuum) or zero (sources). The load is \( \ell(v) = \int_\Omega f\cdot v + \int_\Omega g\cdot\nabla\times v \); the curl source \( g \) carries the term \( -\nabla\times\big((\mu^{-1} - \mu_b^{-1})\nabla\times E^{inc}\big) \) of the scattered-field formulation in weak (integrated-by-parts) form, see docs/theory/maxwell.md#scattered-field-formulation.
