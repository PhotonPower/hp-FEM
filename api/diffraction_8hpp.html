Detailed Description

Diffraction orders of Bloch-periodic structures (2D): on a line \( x = x_0 \) in a homogeneous region the field is a sum of plane waves \( E = \sum_m A_m\,e^{i k_{y,m} y} \) with \( k_{y,m} = k_{y,0} + 2\pi m/a \), and the Fourier coefficients give the reflected / transmitted diffraction efficiencies \( \eta_m = \mathrm{Re}(k_{x,m})\,|A_m|^2 / (k_{x,0}^{inc}|E_0|^2) \) with \( k_{x,m} = \sqrt{k^2 n^2 - k_{y,m}^2} \). See docs/theory/maxwell.md#post-processing-quantities.
