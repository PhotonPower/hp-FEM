Detailed Description

Far-field pattern of the scattered 2.5D field sampled on a closed surface in the lossless homogeneous background (normal outwards). With \( k = k_0n \), the transverse wavenumber \( k_t = \sqrt{k^2-\beta^2} \), the direction \( \hat k = (k_t\cos\varphi, k_t\sin\varphi, \beta)/k \) and the equivalent currents \( N = \oint (n\times H)e^{-ik_t\hat\varphi\cdot x'}ds \), \( L = -\oint (n\times E)e^{-ik_t\hat\varphi\cdot x'}ds \) (in-plane normal n), the field is \( E \to F(\varphi)\,e^{ik_t\rho}/\sqrt\rho\,e^{i\beta z} \) with \( F = \tfrac{k}{4}\sqrt{\tfrac{2}{\pi k_t}}e^{-i\pi/4}\big(\hat k\times L - Z N_\perp\big) \), \( N_\perp = N - (N\cdot\hat k)\hat k \) (the 3D Stratton–Chu far field integrated along z). The radiated power per unit length is \( \tfrac{k_t}{k}\oint |F|^2/(2Z)\,d\varphi \). At β = 0 with an in-plane field it agrees with FarField<2>.

Exceptions
InvalidArgumentfor a lossy background, \( |\beta| \ge k \) or an empty surface.
