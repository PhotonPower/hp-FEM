Detailed Description

Riesz projection of the solutions of a pencil for several sources and functionals: the engine behind RieszProjection<Dim> and AxisymmetricRieszProjection.

The power a current source emits, \( Q(x) = -\tfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{factor}\, \overline{b_J}^\top x = -\tfrac12\,\mathrm{factor}\int E\cdot J^* \); the physical power is its real part, and the real parts of the modal contributions add up to it exactly.
