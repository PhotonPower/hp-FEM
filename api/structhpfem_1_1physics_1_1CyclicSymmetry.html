Detailed Description

template<int Dim>
struct hpfem::physics::CyclicSymmetry< Dim >

Cyclic symmetry of order n: rotations by multiples of 2π/n about the axis through center (3D: with direction axis, right-handed; 2D: counter-clockwise, axis unused). The sector mesh has two sides on half-planes from the axis: master (facet tag of the first side) and slave (the facets of the master side rotated by 2π/n).

With inversion (3D only, order 2) the generator is the point inversion \( x \mapsto 2c - x \) instead (R = −I): the reduced domain is the half-space \( (x - c)\cdot\text{axis} \ge 0 \), axis is the normal of the cut plane, and the two sides are the halves of the cut plane that the inversion swaps (tag_sector_sides). The mesh of the cut plane must be symmetric under the inversion and have a vertex at c. A structure with a C_2 axis or a mirror plane is better reduced by those (they also halve, and combine); the inversion is the reduction left for a structure with only a centre of symmetry, e.g. a general anisotropic body that is point-symmetric.
