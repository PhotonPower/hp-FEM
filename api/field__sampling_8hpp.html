Detailed Description

Vectorised evaluation of scattering solutions for maps and exports (M15 F3): the total or scattered field at many physical points in parallel (point location and basis evaluation in C++, points outside a Bloch-periodic unit cell mapped back with the Bloch phase, points on an interface resolved to the cell above or below), and the field on the n-fold subdivided mesh as a triangulation (points, simplices, values, parent cell and tag per simplex) ready for matplotlib.tri or any other viewer. Convention exp(-iωt).
