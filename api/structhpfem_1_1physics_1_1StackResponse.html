Detailed Description

Plane-wave response of an AnisotropicStack for the two incident polarisations s, p of unit |E| (s: E along \( \hat s = \hat z\times\hat k_\parallel \), p: E along \( \hat s\times\hat k \), so that H is along +ŝ as in LayerStack).

◆ substrate_modes

std::array<TangentialField, 2> hpfem::physics::StackResponse::substrate_modes

Tangential fields of the downward substrate modes, each with unit |E|: s and p of an isotropic substrate; ordinary then extraordinary of a uniaxial one; the eigenmodes of the downward block of a biaxial one. Where the two modes coincide (k along an optic axis) the basis of the subspace that diagonalises the cross power flux (power-orthogonal modes).
