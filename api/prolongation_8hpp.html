Detailed Description

Transfer of a discrete function to the mesh of a refinement step (mesh::AdaptiveMesh): the coefficients on the new DoF map are the hierarchical interpolant (interpolate) of the old function, which is exact because the spaces are nested (same or higher order on the children). Serves as the initial guess of iterative solvers and for comparing solutions across refinement steps.
