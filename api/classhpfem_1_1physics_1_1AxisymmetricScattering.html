Detailed Description

Assembles the order-m operator \( S - k_0^2 M \) with the PML and the volume source and solves for the scattered field. PEC walls impose the scattered field −E^inc only through the homogeneous condition (the incident field must vanish there, as for closed walls of a resonator); open problems have no PEC.
