Matrix hpfem::physics::KeptFactorisation::solve_many ( const Matrix &  loads,
const Matrix &  system_loads 
) const

Tangent solves with an additional load on the factorised unknowns, added after the constraints: \( P^H A P x = P^H r + g \), \( s = P x \). The extra term carries the derivative of the constraints, \( g = (\partial_\theta P)^H \rho_0 \), when a parameter changes them (the Bloch phases under a change of frequency or angle, conical_parameter_tangent). Entries of eliminated Dirichlet unknowns are ignored.

Exceptions
InvalidArgumentif the sizes do not match (system_loads: size() rows, the columns of loads).
