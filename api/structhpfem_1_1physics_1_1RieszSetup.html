◆ background_aspect

Real hpfem::physics::RieszSetup::background_aspect = 1.0

Imaginary semi-axis of the background ellipse as a fraction of the real one (1: a circle); a flat ellipse encloses fewer of the heavily damped (PML) poles, which would otherwise all have to be listed in poles, but must still enclose the modes of interest.
