Detailed Description

Temperature-dependent permittivity, the feedback loop of the optical heating: the absorbed power of the scattering solution heats the structure (Thermal), the permittivity of every cell follows its temperature, \( \varepsilon_r(T) = \varepsilon_r(T_0) + \frac{d\varepsilon_r}{dT}\,(T - T_0) \) (complex thermo-optic coefficient per material tag, evaluated at the cell centroid and stored as a per-cell override of the MaterialMap), and the scattering problem is solved again until the temperature settles (fixed-point iteration with optional under-relaxation). Convention exp(−iωt), SI units. See docs/theory/multiphysics.md.
