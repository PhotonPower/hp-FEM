Detailed Description

Isotropic, non-dispersive materials given by relative permittivity and permeability (complex; with the exp(−iωt) convention lossy media have Im εr > 0), and their assignment to the cells of a mesh by tag. Dispersion models (Drude, Lorentz, tabulated n, k) and anisotropy follow later (roadmap M7/M8).
