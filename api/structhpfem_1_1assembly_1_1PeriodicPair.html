Detailed Description

template<int Dim>
struct hpfem::assembly::PeriodicPair< Dim >

One periodic direction: the facets tagged slave are the facets tagged master translated by shift (the lattice vector a), and \( E_{slave} = \text{phase}\cdot E_{master} \) with \( \text{phase} = e^{i k\cdot a} \) (1 for plain periodicity).
