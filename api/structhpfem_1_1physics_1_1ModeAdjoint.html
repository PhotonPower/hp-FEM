Detailed Description

Left eigenvector of a mode in full DoF coefficients, \( \tilde y = \bar P y \) (zero on PEC DoFs), and the normalisation \( \tilde y^\top M x \); for the conical block space stacked (in-plane | scaled longitudinal).
