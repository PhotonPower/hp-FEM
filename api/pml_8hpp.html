Detailed Description

Perfectly matched layers as complex coordinate stretching around an axis-aligned interior box: outside the box every coordinate ξ is continued as \( \tilde\xi = \xi + i\int\hat\sigma(\xi)\,d\xi \) with the dimensionless profile \( \hat\sigma = \sigma/\omega \), so that outgoing waves decay (convention exp(−iωt); the imaginary part is plus). The stretching enters the Maxwell forms as the anisotropic material tensors \( \tilde\varepsilon = \det\Lambda\,\Lambda^{-1}\varepsilon \Lambda^{-T} \), \( \tilde\mu^{-1} = \Lambda\,\mu^{-1}\Lambda^{T}/\det\Lambda \) with \( \Lambda = \mathrm{diag}(s_\xi) \), \( s_\xi = 1 + i\hat\sigma_\xi \); nothing else in the assembly changes. Theory and profile: docs/theory/pml.md.
