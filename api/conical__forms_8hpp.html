Detailed Description

Maxwell forms of a z-invariant structure for a field with the dependence \( e^{i\beta z} \) (conical incidence, 2.5D): \( E = (E_x, E_y, E_z)(x, y)\,e^{i\beta z} \) on the 2D mesh with the in-plane components \( E_t = (E_x, E_y) \) in the Nédélec space and the scaled longitudinal component \( v = -iE_z \) in the H1 space of the same order. The curl of the mode is \( \nabla\times E = \big(\partial_yE_z - i\beta E_y,\ i\beta E_x - \partial_xE_z, \ \partial_xE_y - \partial_yE_x\big) = \big(i(\partial_y v - \beta E_y),\ -i(\partial_x v - \beta E_x),\ \nabla_t\times E_t\big) \), so that with diagonal tensors in (x, y, z) the bilinear forms read (test functions not conjugated)

\[ a(E, V) = \int \Big[ \mu_x^{-1}(\partial_y v - \beta E_y)(\partial_y w - \beta V_y) + \mu_y^{-1}(\partial_x v - \beta E_x)(\partial_x w - \beta V_x) + \mu_z^{-1}(\nabla_t\times E_t)(\nabla_t\times V_t) \Big] dx\,dy , \]

\[ b(E, V) = \int \big[ \varepsilon_x E_xV_x + \varepsilon_y E_yV_y + \varepsilon_z v w \big] dx\,dy , \]

real symmetric for lossless media, complex symmetric otherwise; the stretched tensors of a 2D PML, \( \Lambda = \mathrm{diag}(s_y/s_x,\ s_x/s_y,\ s_xs_y) \), fit. At β = 0 the forms decouple into the in-plane (H_z polarisation) block of maxwell_forms.hpp and the scalar E_z block \( \int \mu_t^{-1}\nabla v\cdot\nabla w - k_0^2\varepsilon_z vw \). The gradient of a potential, \( \nabla(\psi e^{i\beta z}) = (\nabla_t\psi,\ v = \beta\psi) \), is exactly representable: \( K_\beta = [G;\ \beta I] \) spans the kernel of a(·,·). Sources are paired as \( \int f_xV_x + f_yV_y + f_v w \) with the scaled \( f_v = -if_z \). Convention exp(-iωt). See docs/theory/maxwell.md#conical-incidence.
