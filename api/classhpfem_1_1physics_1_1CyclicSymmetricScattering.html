Detailed Description

template<int Dim>
class hpfem::physics::CyclicSymmetricScattering< Dim >

Scattering problem on one sector of a structure with cyclic symmetry: one Scattering per azimuthal order with a non-vanishing excitation, the fields of the full domain and its cross-sections, absorbed power and far field. The setup describes the sector (materials, PML, the outer PEC walls); the side tags must not be in pec_tags or incident_tags.
