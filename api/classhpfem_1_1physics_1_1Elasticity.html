The mesh with its geometry moved by scale · Re u: move_nodes(mesh, mesh_velocity(u), scale) on a second-order mesh or for p = 1. An affine mesh with p ≥ 2 is first raised to geometry order 2 (edge nodes at the midpoints), so that the quadratic part of u moves the edges too. E.g. for an optical solve on the shrunk part (ADR-0016 §6).

Energy-norm error \( |||u - u_h|||=\big(\int\varepsilon(e) : C : \varepsilon(e)\big)^{1/2} \) [J^{1/2}] (2D: per metre of depth; axisymmetric: with the hoop strain and the weight r, per radian) against the exact displacement and its gradient \( (\nabla u)_{ij} = \partial_j u_i \) (the displacement only enters the hoop strain). Quadrature of degree 2p + extra_order (+2 on curved cells).

Residual a-posteriori error indicators of u in the energy norm (adaptivity/elasticity_estimator.hpp, M20 S5b): the equilibrium residual in the cells, the traction jumps across interior facets (hanging ones included) and the traction residual on the natural boundary (the traction-free facets, the tractions, and the free components of the displacement conditions); axisymmetric problems with the weight r and the hoop stress, the axis without a term. For the hp loop of adaptivity (marking, decision, refinement).
