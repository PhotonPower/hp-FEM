Detailed Description

template<int Dim>
class hpfem::physics::MirrorSymmetricFarField< Dim >

Far field of the scattered field of the full problem from a closed surface of the reduced mesh in the homogeneous background: per part the FarField of the reduced surface (facets on the planes dropped), and \( F(\hat r) = \sum_\sigma\sum_g \chi_\sigma(g)\,e^{-ik\hat r\cdot t_g}\,R_g\, F_\sigma(R_g\hat r) \).
