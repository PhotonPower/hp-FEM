Detailed Description

How the leaf cells of a refinement step relate to those before it: cell i of the new mesh is cell parent[i] of the old mesh itself (path[i] empty) or a descendant reached through the child numbers path[i] (red patterns, one entry per level; the closure may split a cell more than once in one call). old_reference maps reference coordinates of cell i to the old cell.
