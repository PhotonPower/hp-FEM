Detailed Description

template<int Dim>
class hpfem::physics::MirrorSymmetricScattering< Dim >

Scattering problem on the reduced domain of a mirror-symmetric structure: one Scattering per parity with a non-vanishing excitation, the fields of the full domain and its cross-sections, absorbed power and far field. The setup describes the reduced domain (materials, PML box ending at the planes, the outer PEC walls); the planes' tags must not be in pec_tags or incident_tags (they are set per parity). Materials and the layered background must be symmetric themselves (not checked); the PML must continue across the planes, which is checked on them (a pml::RadialPml must be centred on every plane).
