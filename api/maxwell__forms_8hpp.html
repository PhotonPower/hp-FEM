Detailed Description

Element and global assembly of the time-harmonic Maxwell forms on the Nédélec space: \( s(E,v) = \int_\Omega \mu^{-1}\,\nabla\times E \cdot \nabla\times v \) (stiffness), \( m(E,v) = \int_\Omega \varepsilon\,E\cdot v \) (mass), \( \ell(v) = \int_\Omega f\cdot v \), so that the curl–curl problem of docs/theory/maxwell.md reads \( (S - \omega^2 M)\,e = b \) with \( f = i\omega J \) (convention exp(-iωt), ADR-0002; test functions not conjugated). Tensors are complex (lossy, anisotropic media, PML stretching); see docs/theory/maxwell.md#discrete-forms.
