Detailed Description

Mie series of a homogeneous sphere of radius a and relative permittivity ε_r (complex, Im ε_r ≥ 0 for absorption) centred at the origin in a lossless background of refractive index n_b, illuminated by the plane wave \( E^{inc} = \hat x\, e^{i k z} \) of unit amplitude (1 V/m), \( k = n_b k_0 \). Non-magnetic media. Size parameter \( x = k a \), relative index \( m = \sqrt{\varepsilon_r} / n_b \) (branch with Im m ≥ 0).

Coefficients (Bohren & Huffman 4.53, μ = μ₁) with the Riccati–Bessel functions \( \psi_n(\rho) = \rho j_n(\rho) \), \( \xi_n(\rho) = \rho h_n^{(1)}(\rho) \) and the logarithmic derivative \( D_n(mx) = \psi_n'(mx)/\psi_n(mx) \) (downward recurrence): \( a_n = \frac{(D_n/m + n/x)\psi_n(x) - \psi_{n-1}(x)}{(D_n/m + n/x)\xi_n(x) - \xi_{n-1}(x)} \), \( b_n = \frac{(m D_n + n/x)\psi_n(x) - \psi_{n-1}(x)}{(m D_n + n/x)\xi_n(x) - \xi_{n-1}(x)} \); the internal coefficients c_n, d_n follow from the same boundary conditions. Efficiencies \( Q_{sca} = \frac{2}{x^2}\sum_n (2n+1)(|a_n|^2 + |b_n|^2) \), \( Q_{ext} = \frac{2}{x^2}\sum_n (2n+1)\,\mathrm{Re}(a_n + b_n) \) (optical theorem), \( Q_{abs} = Q_{ext} - Q_{sca} \); cross-sections \( \sigma = Q\,\pi a^2 \) [m²]. The fields are the vector spherical harmonic expansions of Bohren & Huffman (4.40, 4.45, 4.50): \( E^{sca} = \sum_n E_n (i a_n N^{(3)}_{e1n} - b_n M^{(3)}_{o1n}) \) outside and \( E^{int} = \sum_n E_n (c_n M^{(1)}_{o1n} - i d_n N^{(1)}_{e1n}) \) inside, \( E_n = i^n (2n+1)/(n(n+1)) \).
