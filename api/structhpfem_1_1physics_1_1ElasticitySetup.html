◆ axisymmetric

template<int Dim>
bool hpfem::physics::ElasticitySetup< Dim >::axisymmetric = false

A body of revolution (Dim = 2 only, ADR-0016 S5): the mesh is the meridian half-plane x = r ≥ 0, y = z, component 0 is u_r and 1 is u_z (no torsion, azimuthal order 0); the hoop strain u_r / r enters, the integrals carry the weight r (the factor 2π dropped: energies and loads per radian), u_r = 0 is imposed on the facets with axis_tag, and the only rigid-body mode is the translation along z. The eigenstrains of shrinkage and the temperature are isotropic (hoop included); that of eigenstrain takes its hoop part from hoop_eigenstrain.

◆ remove_rigid_modes

template<int Dim>
bool hpfem::physics::ElasticitySetup< Dim >::remove_rigid_modes = false

A free body: the rigid-body modes are fixed by six (2D: three) DoFs at three far-apart vertices (the 3-2-1 rule) and removed afterwards by the L2 projection onto them, so that the solution has zero mean translation and rotation (ADR-0016 §4a). Requires loads in equilibrium (eigenstrains always are) and no displacement conditions.
