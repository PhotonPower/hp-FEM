Detailed Description

Residual-based a-posteriori error estimator of the axisymmetric (2.5D) curl–curl problem of one azimuthal order m on the meridian mesh (assembly/axisymmetric_forms.hpp): the residual of the three-dimensional equation \( \nabla\times(\mu^{-1}\nabla\times E) - k^2\varepsilon E = f \) for the mode \( E(r, z)e^{im\varphi} \), measured in the norms of the body of revolution, i.e. with the weight r of the volume element \( r\,dr\,dz \) (the factor 2π dropped as in the forms). Per cell K of the meridian mesh with diameter h_K and order p_K,

\[ \eta_K^2 = \frac{h_K^2}{p_K^2}\int_K\big(|R_K|^2 + \ell^2|\nabla\cdot d|^2\big)\,r\,dr\,dz + \sum_{F\subset\partial K}\frac{h_F}{2p_F}\int_F\big(|[\![n\times w]\!]|^2 + \ell^2|[\![n\cdot d]\!]|^2\big)\,r\,ds, \]

with \( w = \mu^{-1}\nabla\times E_{hp} \), \( d = f + k^2\varepsilon E_{hp} \) and \( R_K = d - \nabla\times w \), all in cylindrical components: for the scaled unknowns \( (E_r, v = -irE_\varphi, E_z) \) the curl of the mode is \( \nabla\times E = \big(\tfrac{i}{r}(mE_z - \partial_z v),\ \partial_zE_r - \partial_rE_z, \ \tfrac{i}{r}(\partial_r v - mE_r)\big) \), and for an unscaled vector field \( (\nabla\times w)_r = \tfrac{im}{r}w_z - \partial_z w_\varphi \), \( (\nabla\times w)_\varphi = \partial_z w_r - \partial_r w_z \), \( (\nabla\times w)_z = \tfrac1r\partial_r(rw_\varphi) - \tfrac{im}{r}w_r \), \( \nabla\cdot d = \tfrac1r\partial_r(rd_r) + \tfrac{im}{r}d_\varphi + \partial_z d_z \). The facet normal lies in the meridian plane, so \( |n\times w|^2 = |w_\varphi|^2 + |n_zw_r - n_rw_z|^2 \) and \( n\cdot d = n_rd_r + n_zd_z \). Interior facets only (hanging child facets against the cell of their parent); facets on the axis and on PEC walls carry no term, the axis conditions being essential. On cells touching the axis the 1/r terms of the residual are rational; the combinations that vanish for the regular solution (e.g. \( w_\varphi - imw_r \) at r = 0 for |m| = 1) do so only approximately for the discrete one, which adds a logarithmic factor to the indicator of those cells (the quadrature avoids r = 0). Convention exp(-iωt). See docs/theory/axisymmetric.md#hp-adaptivity-on-the-meridian-plane.
