◆ pair_orders

bool hpfem::physics::CyclicOptions::pair_orders = true

Solve order n − m with the transposed factors of order m (Scattering::solve_transposed) when the system is reciprocal: always, except for axial Bloch pairs with complex phases (ε tensors are symmetric, ADR-0015 §3). Otherwise, or with false, every order is factorised.

◆ skip_vanishing

bool hpfem::physics::CyclicOptions::skip_vanishing = true

Do not solve the orders whose excitation vanishes: the largest value of the order's incident field and current at the vertices and cell centroids is at most vanishing_tolerance times the largest over all orders.
