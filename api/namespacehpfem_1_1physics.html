◆ axial_plane_wave()

AxisymmetricField hpfem::physics::axial_plane_wave ( Complex  amplitude,
Real  k,
int  m 
)

The x-polarised plane wave \( E_0\,\hat x\,e^{ikz} \) travelling along the axis has the azimuthal orders m = ±1 only: \( (E_r, E_\varphi, E_z) = \tfrac{E_0}{2}(1,\ \pm i,\ 0)e^{ikz} \), i.e. \( (E_r, v, E_z) = \tfrac{E_0}{2}(1,\ \pm r,\ 0)e^{ikz} \). The scattered power of the full wave is the sum over both orders (equal by symmetry).

Exceptions
InvalidArgumentfor m ∉ {−1, 1}.

const std::vector< Real > &  theta,
int  order = 8

Near-to-far-field transform of the order-m field on a closed surface of revolution in the homogeneous background (same formula as FarField<3>: \( F = \frac{ik}{4\pi}[Z N_t - \hat r\times L] \) with \( N = \int J e^{-ik\hat r\cdot x'} \), \( L = \int M e^{-ik\hat r\cdot x'} \), \( J = n\times H \), \( M = -n\times E \)); the azimuthal integration is done analytically with the Bessel functions \( J_m, J_{m\pm1}(k\rho\sin\theta) \), so only the meridian curve is integrated numerically. background gives k and Z; the surface must enclose every source and scatterer and lie in that medium.

Exceptions
InvalidArgumentif the coefficient vectors do not match the maps.

◆ axisymmetric_gaussian_dipole()

AxisymmetricField hpfem::physics::axisymmetric_gaussian_dipole ( Real  position,
Complex  moment,
AxisDipole  orientation,
Real  sigma,
Real  omega,
int  m 
)

Volume source \( f = i\omega\mu_0 J \) of a point dipole on the axis at z = position with current moment moment [A m], smeared over the normalised 3D Gaussian \( g = \exp(-(r^2 + (z - z_0)^2) / 2\sigma^2) / ((2\pi)^{3/2}\sigma^3) \), in the scaled components of order m: the axial dipole (moment along z) has m = 0 only, \( (f_r, f_v, f_z) = i\omega\mu_0 p\,g\,(0, 0, 1) \); the transverse dipole (moment along x) has m = ±1, \( (f_r, f_v, f_z) = i\omega\mu_0 \tfrac{p}{2} g\,(1, \pm r, 0) \). The smearing lowers the radiated power of the free dipole by \( e^{-k^2\sigma^2} \) (Gaussian form factor), exactly for the total power in a lossless medium. Total-field formulation (AxisymmetricScatteringSetup::current).

Exceptions
InvalidArgumentfor σ ≤ 0 or an order the orientation does not radiate.
